The stock fell. The pay went up.

Same CEO, back-to-back years, 2,728 companies, straight from each company's own pay-versus-performance table. When the stock has a great year, the CEO's pay jumps. When it has a terrible one, the pay… mostly stays put.

Reported pay values stock awards when granted, often early in the year, so part of a year's pay is set before its return is known; the pattern is much the same measured against the year before. A new CEO's first year, pay under $100K, and companies whose tables show scale errors are left out. The raises list covers companies with a public float of at least $500M.
