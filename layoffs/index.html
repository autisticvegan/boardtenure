Job cuts are the company's own count or percentage, from its 8-K, 10-K or 10-Q. CEO pay is the Summary Compensation Table total for the fiscal year named, with stock valued when granted; buybacks are the 10-K's repurchases for that fiscal year. Per-job figures are simple division, shown for scale. Companies cut jobs and buy back stock for many reasons; these lines put the numbers next to each other and leave the judging to you. How we know
