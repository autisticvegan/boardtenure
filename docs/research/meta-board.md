# Meta Platforms, Inc. board of directors: current board and changes since 2020-01-01

- **Company:** Meta Platforms, Inc. (CIK 0001326801, Nasdaq Global Select Market: META). It was named Facebook, Inc. until 2021-10-28.
  - IPO priced 2012-05-17 at $38.00 and first traded 2012-05-18 as FB. The FY2012 10-K says the stock has been listed "since May 18, 2012".
  - The ticker changed from FB to META at market open on 2022-06-09 (8-K 2022-05-31; FY2022 10-K). The MVRS ticker announced in October 2021 never took effect.
  - Incorporated in Delaware in July 2004. Dual-class stock (Class B carries ten votes); Meta is a controlled company.
  - The board is **not classified**: every director is elected annually.
- **As of:** 2026-10-04. The newest filing in the EDGAR feed is a Form 4 filed 2026-09-30.

## Conflicts

1. **Sandberg's independence, 2022-10 to 2024-05.** The 2023 proxy's general sentence says every current non-employee director is independent. But the same proxy counts "Seven out of our nine director nominees" as independent, heads her bio "Director" rather than "Independent Director", and leaves her unmarked in the table. I recorded her as non-independent, medium confidence.
2. **Powell McCormick's "Vice Chairman" title (from 2026-01-12) is an officer title.** She resigned from the board on 2025-12-19. The 8-K says she served on the Board from April 2025 until December 2025, and her 2026 Form 3 is filed as an officer. No vice-chair role was recorded.
3. **White, Elkann and Songhurst:** the 8-K and press release are dated 2025-01-06, but the seats were effective 2024-12-30. The Form 3s and "Director Since: 2024" agree with 2024-12-30, which I used.
4. **Kimmitt's Lead Independent Director day.** The 8-K (filed 2020-03-26, his seat's effective date) says the independent directors "have appointed" him. The proxy says "since March 2020". I recorded 2020-03-26.
5. **Some board-size changes are undisclosed:**
   - the 2020 build-up from 7 to 11 and the cut back to 9;
   - the move from 9 to 11 when Arnold and Tan joined in February 2024;
   - the move from 15 to 14 after Powell McCormick resigned.
