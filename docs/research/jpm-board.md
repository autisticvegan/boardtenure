## Conflicts

1. **Retirement end dates.** Bell, Jackson, Flynn and Neal are recorded at the annual-meeting date, per convention. JPM's filings say their terms expired "on the eve of" the meeting, and each exit Form 4 is dated the day before. Their true last day may be 2020-05-18 or 2024-05-20.
2. **Crown's predecessor service.** It is described as "Bank One 1991-2004" in the 2014 proxy and the 2023 memo. The 2020 proxy splits it into Bank One 1996-2004 and First Chicago 1991-1996. His JPM start is unaffected.
3. **Raymond's start.** The 2001 proxy says "director of the Firm or a predecessor institution since 1987". Later proxies say "Director since 2001" plus J.P. Morgan & Co. 1987-2000. Recorded as 2001.
4. **Raymond's exit.** The 8-K says he resigned effective 2020-12-31; the 2021 proxy says he "retired in December 2020".
