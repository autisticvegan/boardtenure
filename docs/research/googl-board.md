## Conflicts

1. **Mulally's last day.** The annual meeting was held 2022-06-01 (Item 5.07), and the 2022 proxy says his term ends at that meeting. The 2023 proxy twice says "until June 2, 2022". I recorded 2022-06-01.
2. **Brin as Chairman.** The 2015 8-K12B and 2026 proxy say Brin was President and Chairman from 1998-09 to 2001-07. The IPO prospectus calls him only President and says Eric Schmidt was Chairman from March 2001. The chair span is kept at medium confidence.
3. **Pichai's CEO start.** The 8-K cover gives the earliest event as 2019-12-02. The 8-K body and press release say 2019-12-03, "effective immediately". I recorded 2019-12-03.
