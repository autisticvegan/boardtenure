## Conflicts

1. **Ellison's founding/director month.** The 1994-2001 proxies say May 1977. The 2003+ proxies, the 2005 10-K and the 2026 proxy say June 1977. Kept 1977 at year precision.
2. **Boskin's start month.** The 1994-2002 proxies say May 1994; the 2003+ proxies say April 1994. Kept 1994.
3. **Berg's start month.** The 1997-2002 proxies say March 1997; the 2003+ proxies say February 1997. Kept 1997.
4. **Ellison's chairmanships.** For the second stint, the 1995-2003 proxies say "since June 1995" and the 2026 proxy says "May 1995". For the first, the dates are April 1990-September 1992 vs May 1990-October 1992. Kept the years.
5. **2014 transition day.** The 8-K body says the appointments happened 2014-09-17; the cover's earliest-event date is 2014-09-18. Kept 2014-09-17.
6. **Ablo's announcement.** The 8-K says Oracle announced her election 2022-03-22; the press-release exhibit is dated 2022-03-24. Kept 2022-03-22 (the Form 3 event date agrees).
7. **Form 4 fields lag the 2025 transition.** Catz's 2025-10-23 Form 4 still says "Chief Executive Officer". Magouyrk's and Sicilia's Forms 4 never mark the director box. Kept the 8-K and proxy facts.
