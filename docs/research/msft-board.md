## Conflicts between sources

1. **2023 annual-meeting date.** The 8-K body says the meeting was held "On December 7, 2022". The cover date and both the 2023 and 2024 proxies give 2023-12-07. 2023-12-07 is used.
2. **Thompson's middle initial.** The 2012 appointment 8-K says "John E. Thompson". His Form 3 says THOMPSON JOHN WENDELL, and all other filings say John W. Thompson. It is the same person (same CIK), so the 8-K spelling is recorded as an alias.
3. **End of Thompson's chair role.** The role ended 2021-06-16 (8-K and 2021 proxy). The 2021 proxy also says his chair compensation continued through 2021-06-30. That is a pay detail, so the role end stays 2021-06-16.
