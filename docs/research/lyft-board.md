## Conflicts between sources

1. **Day of Aggarwal's 2025 chair appointment.** The 8-K cover gives the "earliest event reported" as 2025-08-13. Green's resignation was effective 2025-08-14, and both the 8-K Item 5.02 and the press release are dated 2025-08-14. The 2026 proxy says only "August 2025". I recorded this at month precision.
2. **Start of Zimmer's Vice Chair role.** The 2023-03-27 press release says the founders move into "non-executive roles as chair and vice chair ... effective April 17, 2023 and June 30, 2023". But the prospectus and every proxy say Zimmer had been Vice Chair since January 2019, and the 8-K says he "will continue to serve as Vice Chair". June 30, 2023 is when he stopped being an executive. I kept January 2019 as the start.
3. **Founding year.** Green's "Director Since" is 2007, and the company was incorporated in 2007 as Bounder Web, Inc. The 2025 press-release boilerplate says Lyft was "Founded in 2012", which is the Lyft brand launch. I kept 2007.
4. **Board-size bookkeeping has gaps.** The 2023 proxy says the board would be nine after the 2023 meeting. Yet the October 2023 8-K reduces it "from ten to eight", which implies an undisclosed increase when Whiteside joined. No filing states the increases for Cohen, Risher, Stephenson, Stevenson, Beggs, Hersman or Minicucci. Only disclosed size changes are recorded as `BOARD_SIZE_CHANGE` events.

## Method (exact)

1. **Filing index.** Fetched `https://data.sec.gov/submissions/CIK0001759509.json` on 2026-10-04. It lists 871 filings, `filings.files` is empty so there are no extra pages, and it runs 2018-12-06 to 2026-10-02.
2. **Fetching and caching.** Used `curl` with `User-Agent: BoardTenure research@example.com` and `Accept-Encoding: gzip, deflate --compressed`, sleeping 0.3 s between requests. Each document went to `data/raw/sec/1759509/<accession>/<filename>` and was skipped if already cached. Form 3/4 primary documents were fetched as raw XML (the `xslF345X0n/` prefix stripped).
3. **Documents read in full** (board sections):
   - S-1 (2019-03-01), S-1/A (2019-03-18, 2019-03-27), 424B4 (2019-03-29).
   - DEF 14A for 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024 and 2026.
   - For 2025 there is no DEF 14A. The annual proxy was the contested DEFC14A (2025-04-24), plus the DEFR14A (2025-05-23).
   - Engine Capital's PREC14A, DEFC14A and DFAN14A.
4. **8-Ks.** All 86 8-Ks and 4 8-K/As were downloaded. All 27 with Item 5.02 were read, and so was every 8-K Item 5.07 (2020–2026). EDGAR full-text search for "Departure of Directors" returned exactly the same 27 filings (cached under `efts/`).
5. **Insider filings.** Parsed all 30 Form 3s for `rptOwnerCik`, `rptOwnerName`, `periodOfReport` and the relationship flags, plus all 24 Form 4s filed 2026-06-01 to 2026-10-02.
   - The IPO Form 3s carry `periodOfReport` 2019-03-28. That is the registration date, **not** a board start date, so they are used only for CIKs.
6. **Cross-checks.**
   - 10-K signature pages for FY2019–FY2025: every signing director matches the reconstruction for that date.
   - 10-Qs for Q3 2025, Q1 2026 and Q2 2026 (Item 5 trading-plan disclosures): no board changes.
7. **Quote verification.** Every quote was checked by script against the cached file.
   - **HTML:** `html.unescape`. Remove `<script>`/`<style>`/`<ix:header>` and comments. Replace block tags (`p, div, br, tr, td, th, li, table, h1-6, ul, ol, hr, title, section`) with a space. Delete all other tags. Map NBSP to a space, then collapse whitespace. The quote must be a substring of the result.
   - **Table quotes** (`quote_from_table: true`, e.g. proxy "Director Since" rows and vote tallies) depend on block tags becoming whitespace.
   - **Entity decoding is required.** 34 quotes contain curly quotes or apostrophes that come from `&#8217;`/`&#8220;` entities.
   - **XML:** the quote (the reporting-owner name) must appear in the tag-stripped text, and `xml_fields` (`rptOwnerCik`, `rptOwnerName`, `periodOfReport`) must equal the parsed element values.
   - All 202 pass (176 HTML, 26 XML). 151 of the 176 HTML quotes also pass under the naive "delete every tag" and "every tag → space" normalizations. The other 25 are table rows, or prose where an inline tag such as a footnote superscript splits a word.
8. **Dating rules.**
   - Post-IPO joins and resignations use the 8-K effective date. The announcement date is recorded separately as the 8-K filing date (or the press-release date).
   - Did-not-stand exits use the 8-K 5.07 meeting date.
   - Pre-IPO starts use the disclosed month or year, never the IPO date.
   - Nothing was inferred beyond these rules. Every inferred or aligned value is marked medium confidence and explained in `notes`.
