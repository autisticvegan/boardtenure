## Conflicts

1. **Burns and Thain start month.** The IPO prospectus and the 2020 proxy say September 2017; every proxy from 2021 to 2026 says October 2017. Both are recorded at year precision.
2. **Arora's committees, 2026 proxy.** His bio box originally said "Audit". The nominee table, the committee section and his 8-K give N&G plus Compensation, and the DEFR14A corrects the bio box as a typo.
3. **Advaithi's committees, 2021 proxy.** The nominee table footnote marks her as N&G. The committee section, the summary and her 8-K say Audit only. Recorded as Audit.
4. **2021 board size.** The 2021 proxy says the board "currently consists of ten members", but it nominates 11 current directors, including Wynaendts, who was appointed a week before filing.
5. **Wynaendts at Citigroup.** His 2021 8-K says he joined Citigroup's board "since 2019"; the 2026 proxy says 2016–2021. This affects only the other-boards field.
