The rare exception

Clawed back, forfeited, or left with nothing

Sometimes the parachute doesn't open: pay forfeited on the way out, or money handed back later.

  • lululemon athletica inc. · 2018
    Laurent Potdevin

    No continued or accelerated vesting: all unvested options, RSUs and performance-based RSUs were forfeited, the awards granted in fiscal 2017 were forfeited, and he did not earn the fiscal 2017 non-equity incentive (2018 proxy: "All unvested performance-based restricted stock units and restricted stock units were forfeited.").

    source
  • Wells Fargo & Company · 2019
    Timothy J. Sloan

    The HRC 'exercised its discretion to cancel the Performance Share award in the amount of $15 million granted to Mr. Sloan in February 2019' (311,788 target shares, shown at $16,774,173 at the December 31, 2019 price), and he received no 2019 annual incentive (g244, g241).

    source
  • The Boeing Company · 2019
    Dennis A. Muilenburg

    Boeing's 2020 proxy: 'Of the awards granted in 2019, Mr. Muilenburg forfeited 6,111 unvested RSUs, 5,614 unvested PBRSUs (calculated assuming target performance), and unvested performance awards with a target value of $4,830,867 in connection with his retirement in December 2019.' He received no 2019 annual incentive (no NEO did) and no severance or separation payment (g200, g201, g203).

    source
  • Illumina, Inc. · 2023
    Francis deSouza

    Forfeited all unvested equity and his FY23 variable cash pay (VCP) payout on departure, including the 2023 awards reported in the Summary Compensation Table at $13,969,376 grant-date value (quoted in evidence).

    source
  • DocuSign, Inc. · 2022
    Dan Springer

    No exit pay disclosed; the filing: “accepted the resignation of Dan Springer from his position as the Company’s President and Chief Executive Officer, effective June 20, 2022”

    source
  • Twilio Inc. · 2024
    Jeff Lawson

    All unvested PSUs he held at his January 12, 2024 termination were forfeited (quoted in evidence).

    source
  • Match Group, Inc. · 2022
    Sharmistha (Shar) Dubey

    She agreed to relinquish and forfeit the equity awards granted to her in February 2022 as CEO compensation, and none of her PSUs vested at resignation (quoted in evidence).

    source
  • Opendoor Technologies Inc. · 2025
    Carrie Wheeler

    Forfeited 1,050,000 of the time-based RSUs granted in 2025 and all of her 2025 PRSUs (quoted in evidence).

    source
  • Fidelity National Information Services, Inc. · 2022
    Gary A. Norcross

    Under the separation agreement he waived any claim to a $10 million RSU award that was to be granted for his planned Executive Chairman role (quoted in evidence).

    source
  • UiPath, Inc. · 2024
    Robert Enslin

    Forfeited all then-unvested equity, including his fiscal 2025 annual stock award (grant-date value $13,832,589 in the Summary Compensation Table), and the chance to earn his fiscal 2025 bonus (quoted in evidence).

    source
  • GitLab Inc. · 2024
    Sytse (Sid) Sijbrandij

    His May 2021 Former CEO PSU Award terminated on his resignation; the pay-versus-performance reconciliation deducts $124,125,000, the prior year-end fair value of awards that failed to vest in fiscal 2025 (quoted in evidence).

    source
  • Intel · 2025
    Justin Hotard

    No exit pay disclosed.

    source
  • Intel · 2025
    Christoph Schell

    No exit pay disclosed.

    source

“Estimated” packages come from tables a company files before the exit, such as a merger proxy's golden parachute table; the rest are what filings say was paid. Stock vesting is valued as the filing values it. The $100 figure is the company's own shareholder-return table over a window inside the person's tenure.
